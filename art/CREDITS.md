# Where the game's art and sound come from

| What | Source | Terms (check again before release) |
|---|---|---|
| Card paintings (batch 02: families, Levy, 4 tactics), First Ford battlefield | Generated by Pon with ChatGPT image generation | OpenAI's terms assign the output to the user. Purely AI-made images may not be protected by copyright. |
| Music (7 pieces) and the battle-start bell | Generated by `scripts/compose.py` (numpy synthesis, no samples) | Original to this project. |
| Sound effects (`art/source/sfx/`, except music and conch) | Pixabay (same set as YUDDH) | Pixabay Content License: free for commercial use inside the game. |
| Fonts | Cinzel, Alegreya, Alegreya Sans, Cormorant Garamond via @fontsource | SIL Open Font License. |
