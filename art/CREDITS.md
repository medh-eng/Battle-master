## UI artwork proposed 28 November 2026

Six UI source images in `art/source/ui/` were generated with ChatGPT image generation under Pon’s direction, using the supplied music/SFX/art brief and existing project artwork as references. Exact prompts: `art/PROMPTS-UI-01.md`; delivery and checks: `art/UI-ART-REVIEW.md`. Approved by Pon and merged (PR #7). They are in the game: the crest on the title, the banner at battle start, victory and defeat behind the end screens, march-won at the end of a march, and the icon for the app and browser tab (`scripts/make-icons.mjs`).
