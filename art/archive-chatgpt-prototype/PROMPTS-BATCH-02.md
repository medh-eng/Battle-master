## Review before accepting each master

- **Identity.** Align the recruit and promoted faces at the same size. Compare the brow, nose, jaw and hairline. For Rider, also compare the ears, blaze or other markings actually visible on the reference horse.
- **Upgrade.** Lay the forms in order at 90 px wide and hide their names. The next form should be recognisable from its posture or equipment in greyscale. Check the shared family details again after any regeneration.
- **Frame fit.** Mask the upper corners and bottom 15% with a temporary card frame. Inspect both bow tips, pike heads, pennant ends, sword tips and horse extremities. If an identity cue is clipped, pull the camera back and regenerate before advancing to the next form.
- **Export.** Check that each file contains one opaque 5:7 portrait at 1500 × 2100 px or larger. Keep the frame out of the delivered PNG and use the exact lowercase filename in its heading.
